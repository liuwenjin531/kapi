# 隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

## 中文

**生效日期：2026-11-16**

适用于 iPhone App「卡皮音乐」（含它的分享扩展和主屏幕小组件）。

### 一句话

卡皮音乐不收集你的任何数据，也不追踪你。

### 我们收集什么

什么都不收集。

- App 没有账号，不需要注册或登录。
- App 自己不联网：代码里没有任何网络请求，没有统计、广告、崩溃上报。
- 没有任何第三方代码库。
- 不申请相机、麦克风、位置、通讯录、相册等权限。从相册导入视频时用的是系统自带的选择器，App 只拿到你选中的那几个文件。

### 你的数据放在哪里

数据只在你自己的设备和你自己的 iCloud 里，我们拿不到。第一次打开时你二选一，之后可以在「设置」里切换。两边各有一套歌和歌单，互不影响。

- **iCloud 云盘**：歌放在 iCloud 云盘的「卡皮音乐」文件夹里，由 Apple 的 iCloud 负责同步。
- **本机**：歌只放在这台 iPhone 上的「卡皮音乐」文件夹里，不同步到别的设备。

不论放在哪边，你的歌单、「最爱」、歌词时间微调、歌单封面都存在乐库根目录下的隐藏文件夹 `.kapi/` 里，跟歌放在一起。

此外还有几处只在本机的数据：

- 外观、排序方式、乐库位置的选择、最近搜索，存在 App 自己的偏好设置里。
- 歌曲信息（标题、歌手、时长）和封面的缓存，存在系统的缓存目录里，可以随时重建，系统空间紧张时可能清掉。
- 「正在播放」的歌名、歌手、播放进度和封面缩略图，写进 App 与小组件共用的本机共享区域，让主屏幕小组件显示用，不离开这台设备。
- 从其他 App 分享给卡皮音乐的文件，会先临时复制到本机的共享区域，再由 App 移进你的乐库，导入后清除。

删除 App 不会删除 iCloud 云盘里的「卡皮音乐」文件夹。要彻底清除，请在「文件」App 里自己删除。

### 第三方

只有 Apple 的 iCloud（如果你选了 iCloud 乐库）。你的文件交给 iCloud 同步，由 Apple 处理，受 [Apple 隐私政策](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) 约束。我们没有接入任何其他第三方服务。

另外，如果你在 iOS 里打开了「与 App 开发者共享」，Apple 可能会向开发者提供匿名的使用统计和崩溃报告。这是 iOS 自身的功能，由 Apple 收集和提供，不是 App 做的。

### 儿童

卡皮音乐不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童的。App 没有账号、广告和内购。

### 政策变更

如果以后政策有变，会更新本页并改上面的生效日期。App 如果将来要增加任何收集数据的功能，会先修改本政策。

### 联系方式

有疑问请到 GitHub 提 Issue：https://github.com/liuwenjin531/kapi/issues

Issue 是公开的，请不要在里面贴你的个人信息。

## English

**Effective date: 2026-11-16**

This applies to the iPhone app "卡皮音乐" (Kapi Music), including its share extension and its Home Screen widget.

### In short

Kapi Music does not collect any of your data, and does not track you.

### What we collect

Nothing.

- There is no account. You do not sign up or sign in.
- The app itself does not use the network. There is no networking code in it: no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting.
- It contains no third-party libraries.
- It does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo library permission. When you import videos from Photos, the app uses the system's own picker and only receives the files you select.

### Where your data lives

Your data stays on your own device and in your own iCloud. We cannot access it. On first launch you choose one of two places for your library, and you can switch later in Settings. The two libraries are separate, each with its own songs and playlists.

- **iCloud Drive**: your songs live in the "卡皮音乐" folder in iCloud Drive. Apple's iCloud handles the syncing.
- **On this iPhone**: your songs live only in the "卡皮音乐" folder on this iPhone. They are not synced to other devices.

In either place, your playlists, Favorites, lyric timing adjustments and playlist covers are stored in a hidden folder named `.kapi/` at the root of the library, next to your songs.

A few more things stay on the device only:

- Appearance, sort order, your library location choice and recent searches are stored in the app's preferences.
- A cache of song info (title, artist, duration) and cover art is kept in the system cache folder. It can be rebuilt at any time, and the system may clear it when space is low.
- The title, artist, playback position and a small cover thumbnail of the current song are written to a storage area shared between the app and its widget, so the Home Screen widget can display them. This does not leave the device.
- Files you share to Kapi Music from other apps are first copied to that shared area, then moved into your library by the app, and cleared afterwards.

Deleting the app does not delete the "卡皮音乐" folder in iCloud Drive. To remove it completely, delete it yourself in the Files app.

### Third parties

Only Apple's iCloud, if you choose an iCloud library. Your files are synced by iCloud and handled by Apple, under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not use any other third-party service.

Separately, if you turn on "Share with App Developers" in iOS, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. That is an iOS feature, collected and provided by Apple, not something the app does.

### Children

Kapi Music collects no data from anyone, including children. The app has no accounts, ads or in-app purchases.

### Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the effective date above. If the app ever adds a feature that collects data, this policy will be changed first.

### Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub: https://github.com/liuwenjin531/kapi/issues

Issues are public. Please do not post personal information in them.
